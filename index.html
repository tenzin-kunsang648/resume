About me

Just completed my Master's in Computer Science with a focus on AI and Data Science at Northeastern. My background includes mathematics from a small liberal arts college, software development, and now machine learning. I've worked on enterprise data platforms and medical imaging systems, with experience spanning computer vision, machine learning, software development, and data engineering.

I've also volunteered as a Mathematics Educator for Yindayin, a nonprofit Tibetan organization in New York dedicated to empowering young people through holistic education and mentorship. Each experience has taught me that the most meaningful work happens at the intersections, whether it's bridging mathematical theory with practical applications or connecting technical innovation with education and community impact.

I'm particularly fascinated by the questions that don't have clean answers yet: How do we create systems that enhance rather than replace human judgment? What does it mean to build ethical technology in a world of rapid change? How do we ensure the benefits of these powerful tools reach the communities that need them most?

Currently exploring opportunities to develop computer vision applications that amplify human potential, particularly in areas where visual understanding can make complex problems more accessible. I'm drawn to research that advances our understanding of perception and intelligence, with a focus on building systems that help people tackle challenging problems more effectively.

    Leaving them pleasantly frozen or in angelic motion.

    While the bodies - gently but deliberately - contacted and flowed.
