About me

Just finished my Master's in Computer Science at Northeastern, specializing in AI & Data Science. My path here wasn't straight: mathematics at a small liberal arts college, then moving into software development after graduation, and eventually finding my way to machine learning. Along the way, I've worked across domains from large-scale enterprise data platforms to medical imaging systems. Each turn was necessary and reinforced my commitment to developing technology that amplifies human potential.

My work spans computer vision, machine learning, and data engineering, but what drives me is the conviction that our tools should make us more thoughtful, not less. They should make us more connected to each other and to the work that matters most. In my community work with Tibetan organizations, in the classroom as a teaching assistant, and in the code I write, I'm always asking: How can we build technologies that honor the dignity and innate gifts of every person?

I'm particularly fascinated by the questions that don't have clean answers yet: How do we create AI systems that enhance rather than replace human judgment? What does it mean to build ethical technology in a world of rapid change? How do we ensure the benefits of these powerful tools reach the communities that need them most?

Currently exploring opportunities where I can continue this work: developing AI that serves human flourishing, contributing to research that expands our understanding of intelligence and perception, and building systems that make complex problems more tractable for the people trying to solve them.
